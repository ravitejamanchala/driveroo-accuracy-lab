About and credits

Cambridge Learner Drive is a personal, non-commercial practice project by Raviteja Manchala: short lessons and mock UK driving tests on Cambridge's real roads. It is a practice aid, not a substitute for lessons with an approved driving instructor.

Map data© OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. Roads, speed limits, lights, crossings, roundabouts and cycle lanes come from it; exported with Overpass Turbo.
